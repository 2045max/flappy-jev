The game keeps one request in flight: it posts the bird's position (projected by the last round-trip latency) and the next gap to a Cloudflare Worker, which asks TypeSafe's Jev two yes/no questions: flap now? and flap twice? Orange bar = flap, green = don't. No text is generated.
